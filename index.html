No. ML6 welcomes people at different stages of their musical journey, including beginners.

What instruments can I learn?

We support a range of instruments. More information about the instruments currently available will be added here.

Do I need my own instrument?

Can I come along to the jam?

Yes. The community jam is about making music together and enjoying the experience.
