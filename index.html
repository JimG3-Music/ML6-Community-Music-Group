ABOUT ML6CMG

Music belongs to everyone

✦ ❖ ✦

ML6 Community Music Group brings adults together through music, friendship and community.

Whether you're picking up an instrument for the first time, returning to something you played years ago, or already have years of experience, you're welcome here.

We believe music is something that should be shared rather than kept behind barriers.

ML6 Community Music Group is strictly for people aged 18 and over.

NO MUSICAL BOXES

Every Genre Welcome

✦ ❖ ✦

We don't believe music should be restricted to a particular genre.

Traditional, folk, Celtic, rock, pop, country, blues, jazz, classical, metal, electronic, acoustic — or something we've never heard before.

If you want to try it, we'll give it a go.

Bring your music, your ideas and your enthusiasm. We'll see what we can make together.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Music Should Have No Paywall

✦ ❖ ✦

We believe everyone should have the opportunity to learn music.

There should be no paywall between a person and the joy of learning an instrument.

That's why learning at ML6 Community Music Group will always be free.

We teach you here, free of charge, with the hope and understanding that one day you'll do the same for somebody else.

That's how musical knowledge has been passed from person to person for generations — and that's how we hope to keep the tradition alive.

Learn for free. Share what you know. Keep the music alive.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

✦ ❖ ✦
Who can join ML6CMG?

ML6 Community Music Group is strictly for adults aged 18 years and over.

Does it cost anything to learn?

No. Learning at ML6 Community Music Group is always free.

Do I need my own instrument?

No. Instruments can be supplied for sessions, so you can learn before deciding whether to purchase your own.

What instruments do you teach?

We cover acoustic and electric guitar, bass, ukulele, banjo, mandolin, mandola, keyboards, flutes, recorders, tin whistles, accordions, drum kit, Cajón drums and vocals.

What genres are accepted?

Every genre of music is welcome. If you want to try something, we'll give it a go.

When are sessions?

Wednesday lessons currently run from 6pm to 8pm, followed by the community jam from 8pm to 10pm.
