ABOUT ML6CMG

Music is better together

✦ ❖ ✦

ML6 Community Music Group brings adults together through music, learning and community.

Our Community

ML6 Community Music Group is a friendly community music group where adults can learn, practise, improve and enjoy making music together.

You don't have to be an expert musician. The group is about sharing knowledge, developing skills and encouraging one another.

At the heart of ML6CMG is a simple idea:

Teach what you learn.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

✦ ❖ ✦
Who can join ML6CMG?

ML6 Community Music Group is strictly for people aged 18 years and over.

Do I need to be an experienced musician?

No. People at different stages of their musical journey are welcome.

What instruments are covered?

We cover a range of instruments including guitars, bass, drums and percussion, keyboards, strings, woodwind, brass and vocals, alongside other instruments.

What genres do you play?

We cover a broad range including Celtic and traditional music, folk, rock, pop, country, blues and acoustic music.

Do I need my own instrument?

Please contact ML6CMG to discuss instruments and equipment.

When are the lessons and jams?

Wednesday lessons currently run from 6pm to 8pm, followed by the community jam from 8pm to 10pm.
