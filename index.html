ABOUT

ML6 Community Music Group

✦ ❖ ✦

ML6 Community Music Group brings adults together through music.

Whether you're learning your first chords, developing your playing or simply looking for people to make music with, ML6CMG is about learning, sharing and enjoying music together.

Everyone must be aged 18 or over.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join?

ML6 Community Music Group is strictly for adults aged 18 and over.

Do I need musical experience?

No. People with different levels of musical experience are welcome.

What instruments are covered?

ML6CMG covers a range of instruments. Contact us if you don't see yours listed.

What genres do you play?

We enjoy a wide range of musical styles. There is no need to restrict yourself to one genre.
