ABOUT ML6CMG

Music is better together

✦ ❖ ✦

ML6 Community Music Group is about bringing people together through music.

Our Community

ML6 Community Music Group welcomes people who want to learn, practise, improve and enjoy making music with others.

Whether you're completely new to an instrument or have been playing for years, the aim is to create a friendly and supportive environment where everyone can take part.

One of the ideas at the heart of ML6CMG is simple:

Teach what you learn.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

✦ ❖ ✦
Do I need to be an experienced musician?

No. ML6 Community Music Group welcomes people at different stages of their musical journey, including beginners.

What instruments can I learn?

We support a range of instruments. More information about the instruments currently available through ML6CMG will be added here.

Do I need my own instrument?

We'll provide information about instruments and equipment available through the group.

Can I come along to the jam?

Yes. The community jam is about making music together and enjoying the experience.

How do I find out more?

Get in touch with ML6 Community Music Group using the contact information provided on this website.
